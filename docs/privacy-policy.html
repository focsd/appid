AppId Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

AppId is an Android utility for creating small placeholder applications through the separately installed Termux application. AppId does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information processed on your device

AppId can read the installed-application inventory to let you choose an app to replace. If you explicitly grant Android Usage Access, AppId can display device-reported screen-time and storage information. This information is processed locally and is not sent to the developer or any third party.

Build logs, generated APKs, preferences, and imported Android platform sources are stored locally in AppId's private storage. Optional copies of generated APKs or platform files in shared Downloads remain under your control.

Termux and downloads

When you explicitly request setup, dependency checking, or a placeholder build, AppId sends a command to the separately installed Termux application. Termux may download open-source build packages and a checksum-pinned Android platform archive from the repositories documented in the source code. AppId itself has no Internet permission and does not operate a network service.

Installation and deletion

AppId asks Android to install or uninstall an APK only after an explicit user action; Android displays its normal confirmation screen. You can delete generated APKs and logs from the app, reset AppId's private Termux workspace, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall AppId. Files copied separately into Termux or shared Downloads are not deleted by clearing AppId.

Sharing and third parties

AppId contains no advertising, analytics, telemetry, tracking SDK, account system, or cloud synchronization. The app has no sensitive Android data collection permission. Android and Termux are separate services governed by their own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy before releasing a version whose data practices change. Questions can be sent through focsd.com.
